# Product Focus and Business Scope

- **Primary Business**: Main clothing and apparel retail / group buying (tops, bottoms, dresses, activewear, etc.).
- **Sub-part / Secondary Stream**: Factory surplus and B-grade clothing clearance.
- **Target Category**: Clothes / garments only. Footwear/shoes are NOT targeted.
- **Minimum Order Value**: Not fixed. Configurable in Admin Settings (`/admin/settings`, defaulted to LKR 50,000). New carts snapshot the active setting.

## Brand Partnerships & Dedicated Brand Dashboard

- Partner with top retail and lifestyle brands (e.g. Spa Ceylon, premium apparel brands) that cannot publicly discount their shelf products without damaging brand prestige.
- **Brand Partner Dashboard:** A dedicated private portal where partner brands submit their new discounted releases, seasonal overstock, and clearance lots directly to our team.
- Our team reviews and absorbs these products into GroupBasket (for group lots or promotional gifts), giving brands a confidential, non-public liquidation channel.

## Promotional Gifts & Value Arbitrage (Spa Ceylon Model)

- Acquire brand clearance at ~40% discount (or deeper).
- Distributed strictly as milestone rewards (never sold in open retail), protecting partner brand equity.
- Buyers see the true retail price (MRP), creating high perceived value at low cost.
- Dedicated admin interface to manage gifts, retail values, internal costs, and inventory.

## Personalized Sizing & Body Profile

- Each account is personalized.
- Home page/profile inputs: approximate height, shoulder width, chest/waist/hip measurements.
- Personalizes recommendations and matching apparel sizes.

## Bucket Types & Gift Mechanics (Strict Spend Threshold, NO Automatic Host Gift)

- There is **no automatic host/owner gift**. Gifts are awarded strictly to the person or family unit whose qualifying spend passes the gift threshold.
- **Family Bucket:** Members share the same delivery address. The whole bucket is a single spending unit for bucket milestones (e.g., > LKR 20,000).
- **Non-Family Bucket:** Unrelated members pooling together. No collective bucket-wide gifts.
  - Gifts are earned strictly on an **individual spend basis** (e.g., in a 100,000 cart with 10 people, only the individuals who personally spend > 20,000 receive the gift).
  - **Family members inside a non-family bucket:** If 2 or more members share a household/family address, their combined spend counts toward the threshold to earn the gift. If they exceed the threshold, ONLY that family group gets the gift.
