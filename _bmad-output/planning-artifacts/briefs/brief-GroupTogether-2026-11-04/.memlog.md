- (decision) Required to approve: BR number + certificate copy, owner NIC number + photo, factory address + photos, bank account name/number matching business or owner name. Factory visit to see samples is optional
- (decision) Agreement is fixed-term with renewal (reminder 30 days before end; listings hidden at expiry; paid orders still fulfilled) and a new agreement version must be re-accepted before selling continues

- (decision) On removal (option C): listings hidden and unpaid cart items removed at once; admin decides per paid order to cancel+refund or let factory finish; earned money reviewed case by case

- (decision) Tokens go to each cart member for their own cancelled items (option B), even though only the owner pays GroupBasket (PAY-001 unchanged)
- (decision) Tokens: 1 token = LKR 1, never expire, spent by the holder only on items they themselves add to a cart (reduces owner's transfer); cart minimum counts full item value including token-covered items
- (decision) Quality detection: rating-threshold flag on Factories admin page plus buyer 'Report a quality problem' with photo on delivered orders; removal remains a manual admin decision
- (change) Ratings move from per-factory-per-cart (current FactoryRating) to per product; factory score = weighted average of all its product ratings

- (decision) A 1 or 2 star rating requires a quality report
- (decision) Low-rating report: reason always required; photo required for visible reasons (damaged, wrong item, poor stitching), optional for non-visible (shrank, sizing)
