# GroupBasket area agent model

## Problem and promise

- Buyers: low cost, by removing wholesale and retail markups.
- Factories: time, by clearing sold stock quickly in paid bulk orders.

## How it works (agreed so far)

- Only area agents have baskets, one open basket per factory.
- Buyers pay by PayHere when adding items to their area agent's basket. Tokens apply first; PayHere
  charges the rest. Zero remainder needs no gateway.
- At LKR 50,000 the basket locks, delivery starts, and a new basket for that factory opens.
- Courier delivers from the factory to the agent; buyers collect from the agent.
- Agents keep a LKR 200,000 security deposit, earn LKR 20 per item and hold an exclusive area.
- No cash back to buyers for any reason. Late or duplicate payments become tokens.
- Premium brands stay.
