## 1. Summary for the owner

- Factories can apply publicly or by invitation, with private identity and business checks.
- Approved owners set a password through a separate, single-use activation link.
- Factories accept a versioned 12-month agreement before selling, with renewal reminders.
- New factory listings add the saved commission to the factory price. Only CORE changes it.
- Buyers can use personal tokens on their own items. Fully covered carts skip the bank slip.
- Members mark their items Done before the owner finishes; unfinished members can be removed.
- Buyers rate individual products and submit private quality reports.
- Admins can hide products or remove factories. Affected paid items receive full-price tokens.
- Buyers can request returns within 48 hours. Refusals go to admins for a final decision,
  supported by a private factory discussion. Approved received items receive tokens once.
- Error fallbacks, login rate limits and security headers are built. Sentry awaits setup.

## 4. Rules the owner decided

Source: [DEC-013 and DEC-014](../decisions.md), with the dated [changelog](../CHANGES.md).
These are business rules; examples in planning documents are not commercial commitments.

| Date | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-11-05 | Factories apply publicly or by invite. Require BR certificate/number, owner NIC/photo, address/photos and matching bank account. Visits are optional. Rejected BR/NIC identities cannot reapply. Onboard the owner/manager; they add staff. Private proof is for FACTORY/CORE only. |
| 2026-11-05 | Accept a versioned in-app agreement for 12 months, remind 30 days before expiry, and stop new selling on expiry or a new unaccepted version. Paid orders continue. Existing factories get 30 days to comply. |
| 2026-11-05 | Buyer price is factory price plus commission, default LKR 100 per piece. Only CORE changes commission; changes apply to new listings. |
| 2026-11-05 | Rate each received product once per order. One or two stars require a report; visible defects require a photo. Old factory ratings count equally with new product ratings. |
| 2026-11-05 | Flags never hide or remove automatically. Product flags use below 3 stars over 10+ ratings or three reports in 30 days. Factory flags use below 3 over 20+ ratings or three flagged products. |
| 2026-11-05 | Hiding cancels only that product's undelivered paid items. Removing a factory cancels all its undelivered paid items, ends staff access and sends no factory notice. Buyers receive the reason. Earned money may be marked held, subject to lawyer review. |
| 2026-11-05 | Cancellation/return tokens equal the full captured price, including commission and previously used tokens. One token equals LKR 1, never expires and belongs to the buyer. Spend only on one's own items in any factory's cart, up to the whole price. No cash/bank refunds. |
| 2026-11-05 | Token holds lower the owner's transfer immediately; the cart minimum uses gross value. Unpaid removal, expiry or cancellation releases holds. Payment finalizes spending. |
| 2026-11-05 | Exactly LKR 0 due skips the slip, payment deadline and review. Owner acceptance confirms stock, tokens and gifts together. Even LKR 0.01 keeps the slip flow. |
| 2026-11-05 | Accepted unpaid carts stay frozen when a product is hidden. Confirmation cancels the hidden line and credits tokens. Missed unpaid deadlines release reservations without cancellation credit. Delivered items stay unchanged. Fully cancelled dispatched carts keep Delivery and alert warehouse admins. |
| 2026-11-05 | Members with items must mark Done (`CART-006`/`DEC-014`). Owners may remove unfinished members. Empty members do not block; edits reset Done; Undo and rejoining remain possible. Owner notices occur per completion transition. |
| 2026-11-05 | Request returns within two days of delivery. Factories answer within two days or admins take over. Factory fault means factory-paid pickup; buyer fault means buyer-paid delivery and unused items with tags. Accepted received items give full-price tokens and are marked not earned for future payouts. |
| 2026-11-05 | Add friendly error fallbacks, scrubbed Sentry reporting, per-email login lockout and security headers. Browser Zod may use `jitless` to meet CSP without production eval. |
| 2026-11-06 | Every factory refusal automatically goes to FACTORY/CORE, with a required reason. Admins discuss privately with factory owner/SALES and decide finally. Buyers have no appeal action or appeal deadline. |
| 2026-11-06 | A used/untagged buyer-fault receipt goes to admin review without tokens, including after pre-arrival approval. One final acceptance credits it once; rejection is final. This replaces the provisional buyer-appeal handling. |
| 2026-11-06 | Private return messages allow 1000 characters and ten per minute per author. Post only during admin review; authorized participants can read after the decision. Buyers cannot read them. |
| 2026-11-06 | No sending before approval. The buyer keeps the item until acceptance; pickup booking and receipt are refused beforehand. |
| 2026-11-06 | Completion notices also cover removing, leaving or emptying the last waiting member, excluding groups with no other member items. |
